Quick comparison:
Run Launch-Reconstruction.cmd normally. Choose Auto-place, Start battle, then fire on the upper-right board. P pauses/resumes, F2 restarts, R rotates manual placement. Original game comparison remains available through Launch-Original.cmd. The new prototype bundles eight original assets and does not mount the ISO or write the game's registry keys.

Newly playable:
A complete, finite Classic duel: manual/automatic fleet placement, alternating player/opponent shots, repeated-shot rejection, hit/miss/sunk feedback, victory/defeat, pause/resume, reset and clean exit. Mouse and keyboard controls, resizable drawing, focus-loss pause, held-key suppression on activation, an interruptible sound-effects channel and a persistent mute option are implemented. A separate inspection tab shows three recovered original scenario maps and pauses an active duel while it is open.

Reconstruction evidence:
- The original Classic PCX background and a battleship SPH sprite are loaded by new bounded readers at runtime. Eight packaged assets are byte-identical to the supplied RIP, with SHA-256 provenance recorded in Asset-Provenance.csv.
- Native loader inspection at Bs.exe VA 0x41B230 established the 64x64 x-major SCN order, variable cell payloads, embedded 400-byte records and two record groups per player. 120/121 supplied files consume exactly under this schema. BsSB418.scn is unsupported and rejected explicitly. Entity payloads remain raw, not executed.
- 120 PCX resources decoded. Header/frame spans in 123 SPH resources parsed; IGISLE_0.SPH retains a large unresolved tail and is not claimed fully decoded. Format evidence and limits are recorded in the package's FORMATS.txt and full inventory report.
- Board corner locations are inferred from the original colored border pixels. The managed hit-testing code is newly written.

Modern/provisional design:
Fleet lengths 5/4/3/3/2, touching ships allowed, one shot per turn regardless of result, no timer, and defeat after all ship cells are hit. The opponent uses random search plus adjacent-cell pursuit from visible results, without reading hidden positions when choosing targets. These rules and AI have not been recovered from the original gameplay implementation. Fleet layouts, placement/highlight/hit rendering, surrounding controls, pause behavior and keyboard mapping are new code.

Absent: original movies, combat cutscenes/animations, scenario mission execution, real-time fleet simulation, multiplayer and saves. No controller backend was requested or implemented in this slice. No claim of a completed or behavior-identical port is made.

Implementation and reproducibility:
Windows PE32+ x64 (machine 0x8664), C#/.NET Framework with Windows Forms/GDI+. The existing compiler/runtime were used; no new engine or SDK was installed. Source is split into Assets, Game, Presentation, Audio, MainForm, Validation and Program modules. Build.cmd rebuilds the application; Tools\Build-Tools.cmd rebuilds the asset inspector. Source, binaries, PDB, previews, reports and asset/build manifests are included in the versioned package. Work-in-progress and inspection scratch remain in work\reconstruction-v01.
