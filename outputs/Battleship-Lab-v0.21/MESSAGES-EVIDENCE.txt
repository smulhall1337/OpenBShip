Implementation limits
C# ClassicMessages/ClassicSpriteFont keep loading/formatting/layout separate from
simulation. Current board data supplies phase, shooter and impacted ship identity;
no mutation of Game/AI/movie/ending logic. Font glyph caches rebuild on palette
changes and are disposed before form teardown. Labels/settings outside the
message area, pause/result overlay and lower Opponent label stay modern. Legacy
comparison labels retained. Active captions stay opaque over cinematic surfaces.
Native palette indices/source glyphs are used; modern Windows reserved palette
observations retain their prior provenance. Normal/minimum previews inspected;
fractional GDI+ viewport sampling is not claimed native whole-frame pixel fidelity.
No general/native menu-font renderer, full mode14world loader or new language
support is claimed. Original live Classic gameplay still needs a comparison.
