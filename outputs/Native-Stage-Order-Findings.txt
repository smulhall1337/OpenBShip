Confirmed chronological boundary
A read-only caller check at 00423368..0042337A invokes the opponent prefix
only at phase 4 with 004FB6EC zero. That caller is inspected, not executed by
the probe. The harness sets those conditions before calling original 0041C8C0.

Original prefix chooses weapon/firing movie, then target, then calls original
00423540. Phase 4 becomes 5 and requests view 2. A harness firing-completion
call advances 5 to 6 and chooses travel. Guns consume their extra draw here,
at 0042378D, after real target selection. Other tested weapons consume none.

With media admission returning 0, phase 6 waits until the harness supplies a
separate travel-completion call. With return 1, the original controller calls
itself recursively and reaches the impact endpoint before the firing-completion
call returns. Both paths reach phase 7 exactly once, pass the same weapon,
target, shooter and fleet arguments to the impact callback, and write only the
opponent sentinel. A harness post-impact call requests view 1 and resets phase
and side to 0. Completion calls therefore number 3 and 2 respectively.

The impact endpoint is still a recording stub. The harness supplies completion
boundaries; no original frame clock, playback completion callback, damage,
feedback, terminal outcome or real overworld animation is established here.
This confirms a bounded native controller sequence, not a complete original turn.
Modern missing-media handling deliberately remains explicit pause/skip/reset.
