New reconstruction boundary

Outcomes: 330 miss/no-new-hit, 470 surviving hit and 480 sink, including 160

agrees in this connected path. No runtime changes or new gameplay build were needed.

The oracle records full 100-cell grids and five HP values before/after the shot.
The checker rebuilds the input independently, verifies every final cell/HP value,
roster masks, active-slot changes, movie IDs, random-call counts and final state.
It checks callback-time HP/grid/outcome/roster transitions: damage occurs before
hit/sink movie requests, hit marker8 follows request/cleanup, miss marker1 precedes
miss feedback, and slot removal follows the sink movie request. Existing modern
atomic damage/marker commitment before playback remains a deliberate boundary.

Original travel return1 recurses into impact before firing completion returns;
return0 waits for the supplied travel-completion boundary. Both are exercised.
Non-terminal cases reach original phase0/side0 after a supplied post-impact call.
When terminal callback1 is recorded, the harness stops at phase7/side1 rather
than resetting the controller under a fake game-over lifecycle. The actual
terminal routine, animation clock and playback-completion callbacks are not run.
