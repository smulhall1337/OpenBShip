Intentional modern behavior
The review screen uses existing bounded System.Random fleet generation;
the opponent fleet is generated on battle commit rather than during native
initialization. RNG consumption/cancel history is not original-equivalent.
Drafts isolate edits from the paused game. Invalid drops preserve the old
ship atomically; valid drops update occupancy in one operation. Shuffle,
gold cursor/Start focus, colored validity footprint, keyboard controls and
immediate screen entry are modern. Native fade cadence, dynamic budget/
fleet labels, exact sprite occlusion/cursor rendering and general purchasing
are not reconstructed. PCX/SPH artwork, projection, roster and collision
rules are confirmed individually, not a claim of complete screen fidelity.

Opening Fleet/Back retains the prior paused game; Start commits the draft,
restores recovered rules/current AI/display choices and starts the duel.
F2 discards the draft. The direct Classic duel/manual placement tab remains.
Approved cinematic30percent board transparency and opaque overlays persist.
Original reference acceptance still establishes mainmenu/Quit only. Physical
moving/Start/Quit and audible output require separate user observation.
