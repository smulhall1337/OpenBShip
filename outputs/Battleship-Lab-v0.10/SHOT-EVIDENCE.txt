Battleship — optional battle movie sequence (v0.9, 2026-11-07)

New playable milestone
Before deploying, Display -> Battle movies (experimental) enables original firing
and travel playback in the Classic duel. A shot reserves its coordinate and weapon
without changing the target board. Travel completion applies exactly one impact.
At recovered target settings 2..4, non-sinking hits/misses can then play CL_HIT /
CL_MISS. The turn hands off after that feedback finishes. Sinks and setting 1
omit that feedback, as supported by the traced impact path. Repeated occupied
opponent targets apply no new damage or hit movie; repeated misses can show miss
feedback. Both sides use the same bounded sequencing rules.

The default remains the accepted instant-impact model. The optional setting is
persistent, locked during battle and unavailable in the rotating prototype preset.
Changing it during deployment resets deployment, like other rule settings. Earlier
v0.8 is preserved separately. This is an experimental bounded shot sequence, not
complete original combat animation or an exact port.

Controls and lifecycle
Skip stage advances one stage; Space/Enter can skip while the movie/grid owns
keyboard focus. Other native buttons retain their focused confirmation behavior.
A held firing/skip key cannot skip successive stages. Mouse presses carry a media
epoch, so releasing a press from an old stage cannot click the next stage. P/Escape
pauses, F2 cancels and resets. Paused completion is deferred until Resume. Entering
maps/library, Display or losing focus pauses the pending shot/audio. Returning
requires explicit Resume. Color/mute changes preserve the pending shot. Reset and
cross-game/stale completion tokens cannot apply an old impact. During playback
the board is hidden/locked, then returns with the resolved result.

Missing/invalid video pauses visibly and requires explicit Skip stage or F2. This
differs deliberately from native unavailable-media recursion; no unsupported stage
is silently treated as faithful playback. Sound failures keep the existing explicit
silent visual fallback. The unverified immediate extra CANNON1 effect is suppressed
in movie mode, relying on decoded stage soundtracks. Instant mode retains it.

Random/timing modernization boundary
Travel draws use a third isolated recovered-generator stream (seed XOR 0x721CAB).
This retains accepted target AND firing-choice histories when comparing modes.
The original shares layout/firing/travel/target RNG; its exact whole-game schedule
is still not reconstructed. Both modes match in targets, firing choices, damage
and turn outcomes in paired tests; exact native seeded histories are not claimed.
The player retains nominal 25 fps, modern last-frame holds/audio-clock catch-up,
pause/reset and safe native-buffer ownership. Those choices do not reproduce all
historical timing. Media loading is bounded and replaces only the current clip.

Validation and confidence
61 focused checks and 25 actual-form checks pass, including inherited regressions,
the 528 source phase cases, all media/audio fixtures, delayed exactly-once impact,
paused/stale/cross-game callbacks, full terminal cases and paired instant/cinematic
histories across four settings. UI tests exercise real handlers and live completion,
held keys, old mouse epochs, failed-loader injection, maps/focus/display transitions,
mode locking/preset retention, reset and minimum layout. Events/device failures are
simulated. Two new shot previews were visually reviewed; an actual board/player
layering problem found in preview was corrected. A hidden agent-desktop startup /
clean close and separate SILENT actual-device lifecycle check supplement these.
These checks do not establish physical input, audible playback, perceived sync or
full original-battle fidelity. Large fixture grids are grouped numerical cases.
